Itinerary

Day 0

  • Reach Delhi To Tirthan Valley.
  • Arrival in Delhi.
  • Upon arriving in Delhi, make your way to the designated boarding point.

Briefing

  • Our representative will provide a concise briefing about the tour.
  • Overnight Journey to Tirthan
  • You will board an AC Volvo bus or a private vehicle, depending on the group size and availability.
  • This will be an overnight journey to Tirthan. 
  • Arrival at Tirthan Valley
  • Upon reaching Tirthan Valley, you will check in at your accommodation.

Day 1 

Local Sightseeing (CHOIE WATERFALL)

Check-In:

  • Upon reaching Tirthan Valley, check in at your accommodation.

Exploration:

  • Take time to explore the beautiful hamlet and soak in the natural surroundings.

Visit to Chhoi Waterfall:

  • Embark on a 2 km hike to visit the Chhoi Waterfall.
  • Enjoy the scenic beauty and capture memorable moments.

Leisure Time:

  • Spend a relaxing evening by the river.
  • Enjoy the tranquility and unwind.

Return to Stay:

  • Head back to your property for a delicious dinner.

Overnight Stay:

  • Choose between an overnight stay in a cozy homestay or a camp, based on your preference.
  • Enjoy your time in Tirthan Valley! 

Day 2

JALORI PASS & SEROLSAR LAKE

  • Begin your day with a scenic drive to Jibhi, followed by the breathtaking ascent to Jalori Pass.JALORI PASS & SEROLSAR LAKE
  • Take in the stunning views of the surrounding mountains and valleys.

Hike to Serolsar Lake:

  • Embark on a short hike to Serolsar Lake, a pristine gem nestled amidst nature.
  • Enjoy the tranquility and serenity of this beautiful lake.

Return to Jalori Pass:

  • After visiting Serolsar Lake, trek back to Jalori Pass.
  • Capture more picturesque moments along the way.

Return to Property:

  • From Jalori Pass, return to your accommodation in Tirthan Valley.

Evening at the Campsite:

  • Enjoy a delicious dinner at the campsite.
  • Gather around the bonfire, listen to music, and relish the peaceful ambiance.

Overnight Stay:

  • Rest well, surrounded by the natural beauty of Tirthan Valley.

Day 3

Jibhi Waterfall & Jibhi Cafe

  • Discover Jibhi Waterfall and enjoy its natural beauty.
  • Spend time at Jibhi Cafe.
  • Depart for Delhi in the evening.
Inclusion

Transportation:

  • Delhi to Delhi transportation (likely by AC Volvo bus or private vehicle).

Accommodation:

  • Shared basis accommodation during the trip.

Meals:

  • All meals (vegetarian) from dinner on the 1st day to breakfast on the 3rd day.
  • Trek Leadership and Support:
  • Well-qualified trek leader.
  • Supporting staff and cook.
  • Permits and Safety:
  • Forest permits for the trek.
  • Basic medical kit for emergencies.

Camping Equipment:

  • Accommodation in tents.
  • Trekking equipment, including tents and sleeping bags.
  • Enjoy your adventure in Tirthan Valley! 
Exlusion

Exclusions:

  • Personal expenses and adventure activities are not covered.
  • Anything not explicitly mentioned in the itinerary is excluded.

Additional Costs:

  • If you require porters or mules to carry your luggage, please be aware that this is an additional expense.
  • Local sightseeing in Kasol, if desired, would also be at your own cost.
  • Enjoy your trip, and may it be filled with wonderful memories!
