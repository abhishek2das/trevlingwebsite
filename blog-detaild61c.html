1. Golden Triangle: Agra and Jaipur

Duration: 3D/2N

Highlights: Taj Mahal, Jaipur's Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal

The Golden Triangle is a must-visit for anyone who comes for the first time in India. This classic route combines Agra, Jaipur, and Delhi, offering a perfect blend of history and culture. Start your journey by witnessing the iconic Taj Mahal at sunrise—as everybody knows that it is a part of the seven wonders of the world and You will not forget this site! Then, head to Jaipur to explore the Amber Fort and shop in the bustling bazaars. Do not forget to upload your photo from Hawa Mahal.

Why Choose This Tour?

It is a perfect mix of culture, history, and architecture. Plus, a Best Group Tours From Delhi makes it even more fun as you share the awe and excitement with your fellow travelers.

2. Rishikesh and Haridwar: The Spiritual Escape

Duration: 3D/2N

If you’re looking for a spiritual experience, Rishikesh and Haridwar are must visit. Known as the Yoga Capital of the World, Rishikesh is a city of peace that offers serene landscapes and many yoga retreats. Participate in a yoga class together or try white-water rafting, bungee jumping, and kayaking for a dose of adventure! It is your perfect option for weekend trips.

A visit to Haridwar is a must—attend the mesmerizing Ganga Aarti, although you can also attend Ganga Aarti in the evening at the famous Triveni ghat at Rishikesh where the Ganges River comes alive with light and sound. The spiritual energy here is palpable, making it a transformative experience.

Why Choose This Tour?

This trip is ideal for those interested in wellness and spirituality. You leave feeling rejuvenated, both physically and mentally.

3. Chandrashila Trek: A Majestic Himalayan Experience

Duration: 3D/2N

Highlights: Chandrashila Summit, Tungnath Temple, breathtaking views

You can add this to your bucket list for upcoming weekend trips, the Chandrashila Trek. This trek offers a memorable experience, situated in the Chopta valley in the Rudraprayag district of Uttarakhand. The trek takes you through lush forests and picturesque landscapes, guiding you to the summit of Chandrashila at 13,000 feet. Along the way, you can visit the ancient Tungnath Temple, which is one of the highest Shiva temples in the world and is also known as the third Kedar out of Panch Kedar. At the top, you’ll get a 360^ view with scenic views of the greater Himalayas, including mounts like Nanda Devi and Trishul. The trek delivers a thrilling mix of adventure, spirituality, and stunning scenery.

Why Choose This Trek?

Perfect for groups seeking adventure and natural beauty, this trek fosters fellowship while pushing your limits and creating lasting memories.

4. Discover Chakrata: A Hidden Gem

Duration: 1N/2D

Highlights: Tiger Falls, Deodar forests, Moila top, Chirmiri top

"If you're seeking a peaceful getaway from the chaos of urban life, Chakrata is the perfect destination for a weekend trips. Nestled in the Himalayas, here you will find DJs, bonfires, parties, friends, and lots of memories. If you're short on time, you should consider this for your weekend trips.". this quaint town is known for its stunning landscapes and serene atmosphere. Best Group Tours From Delhi often include visits to the breathtaking Tiger Falls, where you can enjoy a refreshing dip in the natural pools.

Chakrata, perched at over 7000 ft, offers stunning views of the Yamuna valley. Developed by the East India Company, it's known for picnics and a military cantonment. Visitors can trek Kharamba Peak, ski, bird-watch, and explore caves. It's an unforgettable destination!

Why Choose Chakrata?

Chakrata is perfect for those seeking peace in nature. It’s an ideal spot for Best Group Tours From Delhi looking to unwind and bond over outdoor activities.

5. Tirthan Valley and Jibhi: Nature's Paradise

Duration: 4-5 Days

Highlights: Tirthan River, Jibhi Waterfall, adventure activities

Tirthan Valley and Jibhi are hidden gems in Himachal Pradesh, perfect for weekend trips and a Best Group Tours From Delhi focused on nature and adventure. Nestled in the lap of the Himalayas, Tirthan Valley is known for its incredible landscapes, pristine rivers, and tranquil surroundings. Enjoy activities like cafe hopping, trekking, and camping along the banks of the Tirthan River.

In Jibhi, visit the picturesque Jibhi Waterfall and take leisurely walks through the charming villages. The area is also great for hiking, with trails that lead to breathtaking views and lush greenery.

Why Choose Tirthan Valley and Jibhi?

It is one of the best weekend trips and is ideal for nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts. It offers a chance to unwind in serene surroundings while indulging in outdoor activities that bring your group closer together.

6. Wildlife Adventure: Jim Corbett National Park

Duration: 2-3 Days

Highlights: Jeep safari, wildlife spotting, nature walks

If wildlife fascinates you, don’t miss weekend trips to Jim Corbett National Park. This national park is well-known for its Bengal tigers and offers jeep safaris for exciting wildlife encounters. Immerse in nature walks to explore the park’s stunning biodiversity.

Why This Tour?

Get ready for an exciting escape for animal lovers and nature enthusiasts, offering a unique way to bond with your group.

FAQs - About Weekend Trips from Delhi

Tell me the best weekend trips from Delhi?

Some of the best weekend trips from Delhi include:

  • Agra for the iconic Taj Mahal
  • Jaipur for a dose of royal heritage
  • Rishikesh for a spiritual and yoga retreat
  • Chakrata for a peaceful mountain escape
  • Tirthan Valley and Jibhi for nature lovers and adventure seekers

How can I plan group weekend trips from Delhi?

To plan group weekend trips from Delhi, consider the following steps:

  • Choose a destination based on group interests.
  • Decide on the mode of transport (bus, train, or car).
  • Book accommodation in advance.
  • Plan activities and create an itinerary.
  • Try to hire a local guide for a more enriching experience.

Are there any budget-friendly weekend trips from Delhi?

Yes, there are! Many destinations like Rishikesh, Chakrata, and Jibhi offer affordable accommodations and activities, while camping and local eateries can help reduce costs.

What is the best time for weekend trips from Delhi?

The best time for weekend trips from Delhi is generally from October to March when the weather is pleasant. However, destinations like Tirthan Valley and Jibhi are also beautiful in summer, especially for those looking to escape the heat.

Can I book a guided group tour for a weekend trip from Delhi?

Absolutely! Many travel agencies offer guided Best Group Tours From Delhi for various weekend trips. This can be a hassle-free option as everything, from transportation to activities, is usually included.
