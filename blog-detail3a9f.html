  • Weekend Getaways for Solo Travel Group Tours from Delhi

Hey! Are you planning weekend trips? At Travel Junction, we understand that life in this bustling city can sometimes feel overwhelming. But don’t worry; we are here! Did you know that Delhi is also a gateway to some of the best weekend getaways for solo travel and group tours from Delhi? Destinations like Manali, Jibhi, Kasol, Spiti Valley, Chopta, Chakrata, and many more are waiting to be explored.

With Travel Junction, you can discover group trips designed specifically for solo adventurers. Our tours provide the perfect opportunity for strangers to become friends, creating unforgettable memories along the way. In this blog, we’ll help you explore the best weekend getaways for solo travel and Group tours from Delhi that promise connection, exploration, and adventure. Let’s get started!

1: Rishikesh: The Spiritual Escape

Duration: 3D/2N

Highlights: Yoga classes, Ganga Aarti, adventure activities

Looking for spiritual rejuvenation? Rishikesh is the place to be! Known as the Yoga Capital of the World, this city offers beautiful landscapes and numerous activities that give you an adrenaline rush. Participate in group yoga classes, try thrilling activities like white-water rafting, and don’t miss the spectacular Ganga Aarti at Triveni Ghat, Parmarth Niketan, and Shatrughan Ghat. Explore nearby waterfalls such as Neer Waterfall, Patna Waterfall, Kalikund, and the Secret Waterfall.

Why Choose This Tour?

This destination is a top pick for weekend getaways for solo travel group tours from Delhi, offering both wellness and adventure, allowing you to connect with fellow travelers on a deeper level.

2: Chandrashila Trek: A Majestic Himalayan Experience

Duration: 3D/2N

Highlights: Chandrashila Summit, Tungnath Temple, breathtaking views

Adventure seekers, make sure to add the Chandrashila Trek to your list of Weekend getaways for solo travel group tours from Delhi! This trek takes you through lush forests to the stunning summit of Chandrashila at 13,000 feet. You can also visit the ancient Tungnath Temple, one of the highest Shiva temples, and enjoy breathtaking views of the Himalayas.

Why Choose This Trek?

This trek is ideal for solo travelers looking for adventure, camaraderie, and unforgettable scenic beauty, making it one of the best weekend getaways for solo travel group tours from Delhi.

3: Discover Chakrata: A Hidden Gem

Duration: 2D/1N

Highlights: Tiger Falls, Deodar forests, scenic viewpoints

If you're looking for a peaceful retreat, consider Chakrata for your next weekend getaway or solo travel group tour from Delhi. Nestled in the Himalayas, Chakrata offers stunning landscapes and a tranquil atmosphere. Enjoy visits to Tiger Falls, go trekking, or simply relax and bond with your new friends.

Why Choose Chakrata?

Chakrata is perfect for solo travelers seeking a nature escape, offering numerous opportunities for outdoor activities and relaxation, making it a wonderful option among weekend getaways for solo travel group tours from Delhi.

4: Tirthan Valley and Jibhi: Nature's Paradise

Duration: 4-5 Days

Highlights: Tirthan River, Jibhi Waterfall, adventure activities

For a true nature lover's experience, look no further than Tirthan Valley and Jibhi. These hidden gems are perfect for your weekend getaways for solo travel group tours from Delhi. Explore picturesque landscapes, enjoy cafe hopping, and take part in activities like trekking and camping along the beautiful Tirthan River.

Why Choose Tirthan Valley and Jibhi?

These destinations are ideal for solo travelers who want to unwind in nature while participating in fun group activities, making them standout choices for weekend getaways for solo travel group tours from Delhi.

5: Wildlife Adventure: Jim Corbett National Park

Duration: 2-3 Days

Highlights: Jeep safari, wildlife spotting, nature walks

For those fascinated by wildlife, Jim Corbett National Park is a must-visit destination. Known for its Bengal tigers, the park offers thrilling jeep safaris and guided nature walks, making it one of the best weekend getaways for solo travel group tours from Delhi.

Why This Tour?

This trip is an exciting escape for solo travelers who love nature and wildlife, providing an excellent opportunity to bond with fellow nature enthusiasts.

6: Kasol and Kheerganga: The Backpacker's Haven

Duration: 4D/3N

Highlights: Parvati River, hot springs, scenic treks

"Kasol is a love for backpackers and nature enthusiasts.". Explore the stunning landscapes, relax by the Parvati River, and embark on a trek to Kheerganga, famous for its natural hot springs. Enjoy the vibrant local culture and connect with other solo travelers in this beautiful setting.

Why Choose Kasol and Kheerganga?

This destination is perfect for those seeking a unique weekend getaway filled with adventure, relaxation, and new friendships.

7: Manali: The Adventure Capital

Duration: 4D/3N

Highlights: Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass, local markets

Manali is a classic destination for adventure enthusiasts. Experience thrilling activities like paragliding, skiing, and river rafting. Don't miss a visit to Solang Valley and Rohtang Pass for breathtaking views and fun-filled adventures. Stroll through local markets to savor delicious street food.

Why Choose Manali?

This destination offers a perfect blend of adventure, culture, and beautiful landscapes, making it an excellent choice for weekend getaways for solo travel group tours from Delhi.

Conclusion: Embrace the Adventure

Delhi is the perfect starting point for solo travelers looking to explore the incredible diversity of India. From the cultural richness of the Golden Triangle to the tranquil beauty of Tirthan Valley, these weekend getaways for solo travel group tours from Delhi are designed to connect you with new friends and unforgettable experiences.

Contact Travel Junction to customize your next adventure and get ready to create lasting memories with fellow adventurers. Your next journey awaits!
