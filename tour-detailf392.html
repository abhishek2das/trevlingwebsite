PANCH KEDAR TREK, UTTRAKHAND.

₹39,999/Per Person
Duration 15 Days / 14 Nights
Difficulty Easy-Moderate
Group Size Any
Weather Cool
Itinerary

DAY 1

Rishikesh to Urgam Valley

Breakfast in Rishikesh:
● Start your day with a hearty breakfast in Rishikesh.
● Fuel up for the scenic drive ahead.

Drive to Urgam Valley via Prayags:
● Embark on a picturesque drive to Urgam Valley.
● Along the way, you will pass through several Prayags, which are confluences of sacred rivers:
● Devprayag: Witness the merging of the Alaknanda and Bhagirathi rivers to form the holy Ganges.
● Rudraprayag: Experience the confluence of the Alaknanda and Mandakini rivers.
● Karanprayag: Observe the union of the Alaknanda and Pindar rivers.
● Nandprayag: Marvel at the meeting point of the Alaknanda and Nandakini rivers.
● Enjoy the abundant greenery, rolling hills, and pristine landscapes along the way.

Arrival in Urgam Valley:
● Upon reaching Urgam Valley, check into your cozy Homestay.
● Take in the fresh mountain air and the tranquility of this remote location.

Dinner and Overnight Stay:
● Enjoy a delicious dinner prepared by your hosts.
● Spend the night in the serene surroundings of Urgam Valley.
● Get ready for an unforgettable journey through the Garhwal Himalayas! 🌄🏞

DAY 2

Kalpeshwar Trek and Village Hospitality

Kalpeshwar Exploration:
● After a wholesome breakfast, set out for the Kalpeshwar trek.
● Kalpeshwar, nestled in the picturesque Urgam Valley at an altitude of 2,134 meters (6,998 feet), is a sacred site dedicated to Lord Shiva.
● The temple houses the hairs of Lord Shiva, which are revered and worshipped by devotees.
● As you ascend, immerse yourself in the rich and diverse flora and fauna of the valley.
● From Kalpeshwar, soak in breathtaking views of lush green forests and terraced fields.

Spiritual Significance:
● Kalpeshwar is one of the five shrines associated with Lord Shiva in the Garhwal Himalayas.
● The temple remains accessible year-round, making it a revered pilgrimage site for devotees and seekers.

Return to Urgam Village:
● After your spiritual journey, retrace your steps back to Urgam village.
● Most of the trek is a gradual climb, allowing you to absorb the natural beauty around you.
● Arrive at your cozy homestay in Urgam, where warm hospitality awaits.

Local Interaction:
● The villagers of Urgam are known for their hospitable nature.
● Share a meal with them, learn their language, and engage in conversations about their culture, lifestyle, and the region.
● Discover the wisdom and traditions passed down through generations.

Fun Fact:
● Kalpeshwar is believed to be a favorite spot for meditation among many sages.
● As you rest in Urgam, reflect on the spiritual energy and natural wonders you have encountered.
● Enjoy the tranquility and warmth of Urgam village! 🌿🏔

DAY 3

Trek from Urgam to Kalgot Village

Breakfast in Urgam:
● Begin your day with a hearty breakfast in Urgam.
● Fuel up for the exciting trek ahead.

Trek to Kalgot Village:
● Today trek takes you from Urgam to the charming Kalgot Village.
● The trail covers a distance of 12 kilometers.
● As you set off, immerse yourself in the natural beauty of the region.
● During the initial part of the trek, you will walk through a dense forest, where the sound of murmuring waterfalls accompanies your journey.

Urgam Valley Highlights:
● Urgam Valley is renowned for its:
● Apple Orchards: Witness the lush apple orchards that thrive in this region.
● Rajma Cultivation: Learn about the cultivation of rajma (kidney beans), a staple in Garhwali cuisine.
● Potato Fields: Observe the potato fields that contribute to the local economy.
● The valley picturesque landscapes include rolling meadows and grazing cattle.


Arrival in Kalgot Village:
● As you reach Kalgot, take time to interact with the remote villagers.
● Learn from them about their traditional way of life, their customs, and their contentment without modern amenities.
● Discover the wisdom passed down through generations.

Overnight Homestay in Kalgot:
● Enjoy an authentic experience by staying overnight with a Garhwali family in Kalgot.
● Share stories, savor local cuisine, and appreciate the simplicity of life in the mountains.
● Embrace the warmth of Kalgot Village and the genuine hospitality of its residents! 🌿🏔

DAY 4

Kalgot to Panar Bugyal Trek - A Himalayan Adventure

Early Morning Start:
● After a refreshing breakfast, set out early morning for the Kalgot to Panar Bugyal trek.
● This scenic trail covers a distance of 14 kilometers.

Uphill Climb Through Dense Forests:
● As you begin, prepare for an uphill climb through the dense forest.
● The air becomes crisper, and the atmosphere changes as you ascend.
● Feel the gentle breeze and the tranquility of the surroundings.

Changing Landscape:
● The trek takes you through a variety of terrains:
● Farm Fields: Pass through farm fields, witnessing the local agricultural practices.
● Rocky and Dusty Routes: Navigate rocky and dusty paths, each step bringing you closer to the Himalayan beauty.
● Steep Sections: Tackle steep sections with determination.
● Meadows: Marvel at the amazing meadows, where nature beauty is at its peak.
● Panoramic Views at the Top:
● Upon reaching the top, your efforts are rewarded with a panoramic view of the majestic Himalayas.
● Let the grandeur of the mountains fill your senses.

Capture the Scenic Beauty:
● Do not forget to capture the scenic beauty around you.
● The Himalayan landscape, varied flowers, mushrooms, and soft grass create a mesmerizing canvas.
● Roll on the soft, carpeted grasslands like a small child, connecting with nature.

Overnight Camping Under the Open Sky:
● As the day draws to a close, set up camp under the vast open sky.
● Amidst vibrant landscapes, enjoy the serenity and majesty of the Himalayas.
● Let the stars be your companions as you drift into a peaceful sleep.
● Embrace the magic of the mountains and the simplicity of life in the wilderness! 🌄🏔

DAY 5

Panar to Rudranath Trek - A Himalayan Pilgrimage

Early Morning Start:
● After a wholesome breakfast, set out early morning for the Panar to Rudranath trek.
● Rudranath is situated at an altitude of 2,286 meters (7,513 feet) above sea level.
● Feel the freshness in your legs as you begin this scenic journey.

Panoramic Views from the Temple:
● Upon reaching the temple, prepare to be awestruck by the panoramic view it offers.
● Gaze at the majestic peaks, including Nanda Devi, Nanda Ghunti, and Trishul.
● The Himalayan vista unfolds before your eyes, leaving you breathless.

Trek Through Varied Terrain:
● To reach the top, you will trek through:
● Thick Dense Forests: Traverse the forested paths, where sunlight filters through the leaves.
● Gorges: Cross gorges, feeling the thrill of adventure.
● High Alpine Meadows: Walk amidst the lush meadows, where nature beauty soothes your soul.

Sacred Ponds Near the Temple:
● Explore the sacred ponds near the temple:
● Surya Kund: Named after the sun god, it holds spiritual significance.
● Tara Kund: Associated with the goddess Tara.
● Chandra Kund: Linked to the moon.
● Manaskund: Reflect on its tranquil waters.
● Mesmerizing and Soothing Views:
● The entire view along the trek is mesmerizing.
● Soak in the serenity of the mountains, the soft grass, and the cool breeze.
● Let the soothing surroundings rejuvenate your spirit.
● Embrace the pilgrimage to Rudranath and connect with the divine in the heart of the Himalayas! 🌄🏔

DAY 6

Rudranath to Lyuti Bugyal Trek - A Journey Through Forests and Meadows

Post-Breakfast Descent:
● After a hearty breakfast, begin your descent from Rudranath Ji.
● The trail leads you through the dense forest and across lush meadows.
● Feel the earth beneath your feet as you move closer to Lyuti Bugyal.

Forest and Meadow Exploration:
● Dense Forest: Traverse the forested paths, where sunlight filters through the leaves.
● Meadows: Walk amidst the open meadows, where the breeze carries the fragrance of wildflowers.
● The changing landscape keeps you engaged and connected with nature.

Arrival at Lyuti Bugyal:
● Upon reaching Lyuti Bugyal, take in the breathtaking views.
● The vast expanse of green meadows stretches before you, inviting you to explore.
● Set up camp and prepare for a cozy evening under the open sky.

Eve Snacks and Dinner:
● Enjoy some evening snacks as you unwind.
● As night falls, savor a warm and nourishing dinner.
● The cool mountain air adds to the charm of the experience.

Overnight Stay in Tents:
● Nestle into your tent, surrounded by the beauty of Lyuti Bugyal.
● Let the stars be your companions as you drift into a peaceful sleep.
● Embrace the simplicity of life in the meadows and the magic of the Himalayas! 🌿🏔

DAY 7

Trek from Lyuti Bugyal to Sagar Village and Drive to Chopta

Morning Start:
● After a restful sleep and a hearty breakfast, begin your day adventure.
● Your first destination is Sagar Village.

Trek to Sagar Village:
● The trek covers a distance of approximately 12 kilometers.
● The trail takes you through a variety of terrain:
● Dense Forests: Walk amidst the lush greenery of the forest.
● High Altitude Meadows: Enjoy the open meadows with breathtaking views.
● Peaceful Surroundings: Capture the tranquility of this extremely peaceful place.
● Be sure to click some good snaps along the way.

Drive to Chopta:
● After reaching Sagar Village, drive 32 kilometers to Chopta.
● Chopta, often referred to as the “Switzerland of India”, lives up to its reputation.
● It is considered one of the most attractive places in the region.
● As you arrive, the exhaustion from the trek fades, and a beautiful fairy world unfolds before you.

Majestic Views and Wildlife:
● From Chopta, enjoy majestic views of the snow-clad Himalayas.
● The lush green meadows, dense forests, and surrounding areas create a mesmerizing panorama.
● Keep an eye out for wildlife:Musk Deer , Pheasant , Monal And more!

Flora and Fauna:
● Chopta offers a rich variety of flora and fauna.
● During the trek, you will encounter numerous Rhododendron and Deodar trees.

Overnight Stay in Chopta:
● After a hectic day of trekking and driving, rest and recharge.
● Spend the night in Chopta, surrounded by the magic of the Himalayas.
● Embrace the beauty of Chopta and let the serene landscapes soothe your soul! 🌿🏔

DAY 8

Chopta to Tungnath and Chandrashila Trek

Early Morning Start:
● Begin your day with a wholesome breakfast and some morning exercises.
● Today adventure takes you to Tungnath and Chandrashila.

Trek to Tungnath:
● Tungnath, located at an altitude of 3,680 meters (12,073 feet), is the highest shrine of Lord Shiva in the world.
● Legend has it that Lord Shiva arms were identified here and are worshipped.
● The temple also houses Nandi, Shiva sacred bull, facing the temple gate.
● Smaller temples dedicated to Goddess Parvati and Lord Vyas complete the spiritual ambiance.
● The trek from Chopta to Tungnath covers approximately 3.5 kilometers and is an easy hike.
● The well-made concrete path winds through lush green alpine meadows and dense forests, surrounded by snow-capped Himalayan ranges.

Chandrashila Summit:
● Continuing 1.5 kilometers beyond Tungnath, you reach Chandrashila.
● At an altitude of 4,000 meters (13,123 feet), this peak offers enchanting views.
● During winters, the entire landscape is blanketed in white snow.
● The journey to Chandrashila rewards you with a majestic panorama of the Himalayas.

Late Evening Drive to Ukhimath/Guptkashi:
● After a day of trekking and exploration, drive back to Ukhimath or Guptkashi.
● Rest and recharge in the guest house.
● Embrace the spiritual energy and natural wonders of Tungnath and Chandrashila! 🌄🏔

DAY 9

Trek from Ukhimath to Gaundhar - A Pilgrimage and Scenic Journey

Morning Start:
● Begin your day with a wholesome breakfast in Ukhimath.
● Prepare for a day of pilgrimage and scenic beauty.

Drive to Ransi:
● After breakfast, drive to Ransi, the starting point of your trek.
● Ransi serves as the gateway to the enchanting Gaundhar Village.

Trek to Gaundhar:
● The trek covers a distance of approximately 7 kilometers.
● As you walk, immerse yourself in the natural beauty of the Himalayas.
● Gaundhar, situated at an altitude of 3,073 meters (10,082 feet), acts as a base camp for trekkers heading towards Madhyamaheshwar.
● Devotees traveling to the Madhyamaheshwar temple often choose to halt at Gaundhar, making this route a popular pilgrimage path.

Confluence of Rivers:
● Along the trek, you will come across a bridge.
● From here, witness the confluence of the Madhyamaheshwar Ganga River and the holy Markanga Ganga River.
● This mesmerizing view is one of the lowest points of our trek.
● The scenic beauty cannot be adequately expressed in literature.

Overnight Stay in Gaundhar Homestay:
● After reaching Gaundhar, enjoy a hearty dinner.
● Proceed for an overnight stay in a cozy homestay, surrounded by the Himalayan landscape.
● Embrace the pilgrimage and the breathtaking views of Gaundhar! 🌄🏔

DAY 10

Gaundhar to Madhyamaheshwar - A Sacred Journey to Lord Shiva Abode

Early Morning Start:
● Begin your day with a nourishing breakfast in Gaundhar.
● Prepare for a spiritual and scenic trek to Madhyamaheshwar.

Trek to Madhyamaheshwar:
● The trek covers a distance of approximately 10 kilometers.
● The route is steep and takes you through dense forests.
● As you ascend, the landscape unfolds, revealing grassy slopes, lush green meadows, and emerald streams.
● Madhyamaheshwar, situated at an altitude of 3,490 meters (11,453 feet), holds a special place in the hearts of devotees.

The Fourth Shrine of Lord Shiva:
● Madhyamaheshwar is the fourth shrine of the Panch Kedar pilgrimage circuit.
● Legend has it that Lord Shiva navel reappeared here after it vanished at Kedarnath.
● The temple, built in typical North Indian style, honors Har-Gouri, Parvati, and Ardhanarishwara.
● Sacred Sounds and Scenic Views:
● Immerse yourself in the holy sounds of the temple bell.
● Set up camp and witness the incredible view of prominent peaks like Kedarnath, Chaukhamba, and Neelkanth.

Dinner and Overnight Stay:
● As night falls, enjoy a wholesome dinner.
● Rest in the embrace of the Himalayas, knowing you are in the presence of Lord Shiva.
● May your soul find solace in this sacred abode! 🌄🏔

DAY 11

Trek from Madhyamaheshwar to Gaundhar - A Himalayan Journey

Early Morning Start:
● Begin your day with an early morning breakfast in Madhyamaheshwar.
● Pay homage to the ancient deity at the temple.

Trek to Gaundhar:
● The trek covers a distance of approximately 10 kilometers.
● Follow the same route as the previous day, retracing your steps towards Gaundhar.
● As you walk, immerse yourself in the mesmerizing atmosphere and scenic beauty of the Garhwal Himalayan region.
● Capture the stunning vistas through your lens, making your trekking experience last forever.
● Dinner and Overnight Stay in Gaundhar Camp:
● Arrive at Gaundhar and set up camp.
● Enjoy a wholesome dinner amidst the tranquil surroundings.
● Let the memories of this beautiful Himalayan journey linger as you rest for the night.
● Embrace the magic of the mountains and the serenity of Gaundhar! 🌄🏔

DAY 12

Trek from Gaundhar to Ransi - A Scenic Himalayan Journey

Early Morning Start:
● Begin your day with an early morning breakfast in Gaundhar.
● Prepare for another day of trekking amidst the stunning Himalayan landscape.

Trek to Ransi:
● The trek covers a distance of approximately 6 kilometers.
● As you walk, immerse yourself in the beauty of the surroundings:
● Local Villagers: Interact with the friendly villagers along the way.
● Water Streams: Cross picturesque streams that flow through the region.
● Mountain Views: Marvel at the majestic peaks that surround you.
● Cattle Grazing: Observe cattle grazing peacefully in the meadows.


Positive Energy and Natural Vibes:
● The entire trekking route exudes positive energy.
● Let the Himalayas infuse your spirit with their magic.


Dinner and Overnight Stay in Ransi:
● After reaching Ransi, enjoy a hearty dinner.
● Rest well, knowing you have experienced the serenity of the mountains.
● Embrace the simplicity of life on the trail and the warmth of the Himalayas! 🌄🏔

DAY 13

Trek from Ransi to Kalimath and Drive to Gaurikund via Guptkashi

Early Morning Start:
● Begin your day with an early morning breakfast in Ransi.
● Prepare for a day of trekking and exploration.

Trek to Kalimath:
● The trek covers a distance of approximately 13 kilometers.
● The route mostly passes through dense forests.
● Along the way, you will encounter sights with evidence of landslides and cold water streams flowing through the terrain.
● Kalimath, situated on the banks of the Kali Ganga River, awaits your arrival.

Visit the Kali Temple:
● Upon reaching Kalimath, pay a visit to the Kali Temple.
● Many devotees come here every year, especially during Navratris when the temple experiences a rush of pilgrims.
● Interestingly, the Kali temple does not have an idol for worship; instead, the Sri Yantra serves as the object of devotion.

Drive to Gaurikund via Guptkashi:
● After exploring Kalimath, take a drive from Kalimath to Gaurikund via Guptkashi.
● The total drive covers a distance of 39 kilometers.
● In Guptkashi (1319 meters), you can visit some ancient temples and soak in the spiritual vibes.
● Finally, head back to Gaurikund (1981 meters) for dinner and an overnight stay at a hotel.
● Embrace the spiritual energy and natural wonders of this Himalayan journey! 🌄🏔

DAY 14

Trek from Gaurikund to Kedarnath - A Sacred Himalayan Journey

Early Morning Start:
● Begin your day with a nourishing breakfast in Gaurikund.
● Prepare for a spiritual and scenic trek to Kedarnath.

Trek to Kedarnath:
● The trek covers a distance of approximately 16 kilometers.
● As you walk, immerse yourself in the beauty of the surroundings:
● Incredible Views: The route offers breathtaking vistas of the Himalayas.
● River Flowing: Observe the river flowing through the valley on the right side.
● No Steep Climbing: The entire trek distance is composed of a walk with no steep climbs.

Arrival at Kedarnath:
● Upon reaching Kedarnath, take in the spiritual energy of this ancient temple.
● Kedarnath, situated at an altitude of 3,584 meters (11,755 feet), is one of the Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva.
● Legend has it that the hump of Lord Shiva appeared here.
● Dinner and Overnight Stay at Kedarnath Lodge:
● Enjoy a wholesome dinner at the lodge.
● Rest well, knowing you are in the presence of Lord Shiva.
● Embrace the sacredness of Kedarnath and the majesty of the Himalayas! 🌄🏔

DAY 15

Kedarnath to Gaurikund and Drive to Guptkashi

Morning Rituals at Kedarnath:
● Begin your day with an early morning breakfast in Kedarnath.
● Seek the blessings of Kedarnath Ji and participate in the morning pooja.

Trek from Kedarnath to Gaurikund:
● The trek covers a distance of approximately 19 kilometers.
● Follow the same route as the previous day, retracing your steps towards Gaurikund.
● The picturesque trail winds through stunning landscapes of meadows, forests, and streams.

Drive to Guptkashi:
● After reaching Gaurikund, take lunch.
● Then drive towards Guptkashi, covering a distance of 30 kilometers (approximately 2.5 hours).
● Guptkashi is a significant town where you can visit some ancient temples.

Overnight Stay in Guptkashi:
● Arrive at Guptkashi and rest for the night.
● Let the spiritual energy of the region envelop you as you prepare for the next leg of your journey.
● Embrace the sacredness of Kedarnath and the tranquility of Guptkashi! 🌄🏔🌟

DAY 16

Guptkashi to Rishikesh/Haridwar - Homeward Bound

Early Morning Start:
● Begin your day with an early breakfast in Guptkashi.
● Prepare for the onward journey.
● Distance to Prominent Places:
● The distance from Guptkashi to:
● Haridwar is approximately 207 kilometers.
● Rishikesh is also a convenient option for your homeward journey.
● Safe travels as you head back from this spiritual and scenic expedition! 🌄🏔
