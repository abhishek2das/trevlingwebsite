TRIP TO MEGHALAYA

₹24,999/Per Person
Duration 7 Days / 6 Nights
Difficulty Easy-Moderate
Group Size Any
Weather Cool
Itinerary

DAY 1

Transfer from Guwahati to Shillong - Visit Umium Lake

Arrival at Guwahati:
● Meet with our representatives at Guwahati airport or railway station.
Proceed to Shillong: Embark on the scenic drive to Shillong, which is often referred to as the “Scotland of the East”. The distance is approximately 100 kilometers, and the journey takes around 3 hours by road.

En Route:
● Enjoy the picturesque landscapes as you travel through abundant greenery, rolling hills, and waterfalls. The mist and clouds add to the enchanting experience.
● Umium Lake (Barapani): Make a stop at Umium Lake, a majestic and placid lake surrounded by Sylvan hills. Take in the serene beauty of this natural wonder.
● Check-in at Shillong: Upon arrival in Shillong, check in to your hotel and get settled.

Evening Leisure:
● The evening is free for leisure. Explore the local surroundings or simply relax and soak in the tranquil ambiance.

Overnight Stay:
● Spend the night in Shillong, ready for more adventures in the days to come.
● Enjoy your journey through the magical state of Meghalaya! 🌿🌄

DAY 2

Shillong to Dawki - Exploring Waterfalls and Crystal Clear Waters

Breakfast in Shillong:
● Start your day with a hearty breakfast at your hotel in Shillong.

Drive to Mawlynnong:
● After breakfast, embark on a scenic drive to Mawlynnong, known as Asia cleanest village.
● Along the way, you will be treated to breathtaking views of lush green landscapes.

Mawlynnong Exploration:
● Tree House: Visit the unique Tree House in Mawlynnong, where you can enjoy panoramic views of the surrounding area.
Single Living Root Bridge: Witness the fascinating living root bridges that connect trees, a testament to the indigenous engineering skills of the Khasi tribe.
● Sky View Point: Climb up to the Sky View Point for mesmerizing vistas of the rolling hills and dense forests.

● Balancing Rock: Admire the Balancing Rock, a natural wonder perched precariously on another rock.
● Bophill Water Falls: Explore the enchanting Bophill Water Falls, surrounded by lush greenery.

Umngot River and Dawki Hanging Bridge:
● Proceed towards Dawki, home to the crystal-clear waters of Umngot River.
● Umngot River: Take a thrilling boat ride on a wooden boat in the Umngot River, where the water is so clear that you can see through it.
● Dawki Hanging Bridge: Visit the iconic Dawki Hanging Bridge, constructed in 1932 by the British. The suspension bridge offers stunning views of the river below.

Fun-Filled Evening by the Riverside:
● Set up camp by the riverside in Dawki.
● Enjoy the serene ambiance, listen to the gentle flow of the river, and immerse yourself in nature.
● Overnight Stay in Dawki: Spend the night camping under the starry sky, surrounded by the beauty of Meghalaya.
● Get ready for an unforgettable experience as you explore the wonders of Dawki and its crystal-clear waters! 🌊🏞

DAY 3

Dawki to Cherrapunji - Adventure and Exploration

Water Activities in Umngot River:
● After a hearty breakfast, get ready for an exciting day of water activities in the Umngot River.
● Kayaking: Paddle through the crystal-clear waters, surrounded by lush greenery.
● Boating: Explore the serene river on a boat, taking in the breathtaking views.
● Cliff Jumping: For the adrenaline junkies, take a leap off the cliffs into the refreshing river.

Mawkdok Zip Lining:
● En route to Cherrapunji, stop at Mawkdok for an exhilarating zip-lining experience.
● Soar above the lush valleys and enjoy the thrill of flying amidst nature.

Arwah Caves Exploration:
● Arrive in Cherrapunji and head straight to the Arwah Caves.
● These caves are known for their limestone formations and fascinating fossils.
● Although only a small part of the cave is open to tourists, it is a treat for adventurers and archaeology enthusiasts.
● Explore the cave, marvel at its natural wonders, and learn about its geological history.
● The cave extends for about 300 meters, and the exploration takes approximately 20 minutes.

Overnight Stay in Cherrapunji:
● Check in to a comfortable resort in Cherrapunji.
● Rest and recharge for more adventures in the days ahead.
● Enjoy the thrill of zip-lining, uncover the secrets of Arwah Caves, and immerse yourself in the natural beauty of Cherrapunji! 🌿🌄

DAY 4

Exploring Waterfalls and Mawsmai Caves

Breakfast at the Resort:
● Begin your day with a delicious breakfast at your beautiful resort in Cherrapunji.
● Take in the serene surroundings and prepare for an exciting day ahead.

Nohkalikai Waterfall:
● Start your waterfall adventure by visiting the majestic Nohkalikai Waterfall.
● This stunning waterfall plunges from a height of 335 meters (1,120 feet), making it the fifth highest waterfall in the world.
● Marvel at the sheer force of nature as water cascades down the cliffs.

Wei Sawdong Falls (Three Steps Falls):
● Next, head to the Wei Sawdong Falls, also known as the Three Steps Falls.
● A short 20-minute hike down the falls will lead you to different levels of the waterfall, each offering breathtaking viewpoints.
● Capture the beauty of the falls and immerse yourself in the natural surroundings.

Mawsmai Caves Exploration:
● Conclude your day by exploring the fascinating Mawsmai Caves.
● These caves, formed out of limestone, are unique in Meghalaya because they are well-lit for tourists to appreciate their natural formations.
● While the entire cave system is quite extensive, only a 150-meter section is open for visitors.
● Witness the intricate stalactites, stalagmites, and other geological wonders created over years of natural erosion and underground water flow.

Overnight Stay in Cherrapunji:
● Return to your resort in Cherrapunji and rest after an adventurous day.
● Reflect on the beauty of the waterfalls and the mysteries hidden within the Mawsmai Caves.
● Enjoy the magic of Cherrapunji waterfalls and delve into the secrets of its limestone caves! 🌊🏞

DAY 5

Trek to Double Decker Living Root Bridge & Rainbow Falls

Drive to Tyrna Village:
● After breakfast, embark on a bumpy 20-kilometer drive to the village of Tyrna.
● The journey itself is part of the adventure, as you traverse winding roads amidst the lush Meghalayan landscape.

Umshiang Double Decker Living Root Bridge:
● Today, we dedicate our time to exploring the fascinating living root bridges of Meghalaya.
● The Umshiang Double Decker Living Root Bridge is the pinnacle of these natural wonders.
● To reach it, you will need to descend approximately 3,500 cemented steps to the village of Nongriat, where this remarkable bridge is located.

Ritymmen Single Decker Living Root Bridge:
● Along the way, after descending about 1,000 steps, you will encounter the Ritymmen Single Decker Living Root Bridge.
● This bridge hangs gracefully over a rain-fed river and stretches for an impressive 50 meters.
● As you walk across it, feel the bridge sway and swing, adding an extra layer of adventure to your experience.

Villages and Footbridges:
● The trail to the double-decker bridge winds through several small villages.
● The locals are always welcoming, and their smiles add warmth to your journey.
● Along the way, you will also encounter a couple of beautiful footbridges that connect the villages.

The Double Decker Living Root Bridge:
● Finally, arrive at the Umshiang Double Decker Living Root Bridge.
● These bridges are created from the massive rubber trees growing on either side of the river.
● The intricate tangle of roots forms a complex and sturdy structure, making these bridges true wonders of nature.

Rainbow Falls:
● The trail beyond the double-decker bridge leads to the natural pools and the famous Rainbow Falls.
● We will definitely explore this enchanting waterfall, where sunlight creates colorful rainbows in the mist.

Overnight Stay in Tyrna:
● After a day filled with adventure and natural beauty, rest at a beautiful resort in Tyrna.
● Reflect on the marvels you have witnessed and prepare for more exciting experiences ahead.
● Get ready to immerse yourself in the magic of Meghalaya living root bridges and the captivating Rainbow Falls! 🌿🌈

DAY 6

Cherrapunji to Mawphanlur via Mawphlang Sacred Forest

Breakfast at Cherrapunji:
● Start your day with a delicious breakfast at your accommodation in Cherrapunji.
● Pack your bags and get ready for an exciting day ahead.

Mawphlang Sacred Forest Exploration:
● Our first stop today is the Sacred Forest of Meghalaya in Mawphlang.
● As we enter the forest, our local guide will share fascinating stories about its significance, traditions, and the rare plants found there.
● Keep your camera ready, as our guide is also a skilled photographer who knows the best spots to capture unbelievable pictures in the forest.
● Gain insight into the beliefs and customs of the Khasi people of Meghalaya through these captivating tales.

Mawphanlur - The Hidden Paradise:
● After our guided tour of the forest, we continue our journey towards Mawphanlur.
● This beautiful valley is adorned with uncountable lakes and offers breathtaking views.
● Imagine gentle meadows, crystal-clear lakes, and idyllic rustic scenes – Mawphanlur is a paradise waiting to be explored.
● The cool temperatures and starlit skies make it an ideal place for an amazing last evening of your tour.

Lakeside Cottages at Mawphanlur:
● Check in to your cozy lakeside cottages in Mawphanlur.
● Enjoy the tranquility of the surroundings, listen to the sounds of nature, and immerse yourself in the beauty of this hidden gem.
● Get ready for an unforgettable evening by the lakeside in Mawphanlur! 🌿🌅

DAY 7

Mawphanlur to Guwahati Airport

Morning Bliss in Mawphanlur:
● Wake up early to witness the beautiful sunrise in Mawphanlur.

Spend your morning by:
● Relaxing by the lakeside, soaking in the tranquility.
● Taking a leisurely walk around the village, enjoying the fresh air.
● Practicing yoga amidst nature.

Breakfast at Mawphanlur:
● Enjoy a delicious breakfast at your lakeside cottage.
● Take in the serene surroundings one last time.

Journey Back to Guwahati Airport:
● After breakfast, pack your bags and bid farewell to Mawphanlur.
● Start your journey back towards Guwahati Airport.
● The drive takes approximately 4+ hours.
● Flight Booking Timing:
● Important: Request to book flights post 4:00 pm only.
● Ensure you have enough time to reach the airport comfortably.
● Reflect on the magical experiences of Meghalaya as you head back to Guwahati. Safe travels! 🌄✈
