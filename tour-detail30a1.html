The Challenging-Pangarchulla Trek

₹10,999/Per Person
Duration 7 Days / 6 Nights
Difficulty Easy-Moderate
Group Size Any
Weather Cool
Itinerary

DAY 1

Journey from Rishikesh to Joshimath/Lower Auli

Altitude: 6,750 feet
Distance: 256 km drive (approximately 8 hours)

Pickup from Rishikesh:
● Trekkers will be picked up from Rishikesh between 6:30 am - 7:30 am in a Traveller/Ertiga/Tata Sumo or a similar vehicle.

Arrival at Joshimath:
● Arrive at Joshimath approximately by 4 pm.
● Enjoy some tea, followed by a briefing of the trek and dinner.

Meals:
● Breakfast and lunch will be provided on the way (excluded from the package).

Accommodation:
● Accommodation will be arranged at the Hotel/Homestay in Joshimath.
● May this journey be the beginning of an unforgettable adventure! 🏔🚗

DAY 2

Trek from Dhak Village to Gulling via Verdant Forests

Altitude: 9,650 feet
Time Taken: Approx. 6 hours
Trek Gradient: Moderate
Water Sources: You can fill your bottles in the Kharchi village.

Breakfast and Drive to Dhak:
● Start your day with a delectable breakfast.
● After a short drive of 10 km, reach Dhak village.

Trek Uphill to Gulling:
● Witness the giant mountains in front of you, including the most pampered mountain known as Sleeping Lady Mountain—a creation of God that leaves you in awe.
● From the meadows, continue trekking through verdant forests thriving with a variety of bird species.
● Reach the beautiful prairies and soak in the natural beauty.

Thickets and Outstanding Views:
● Climb down to arrive at another expanse of thickets with Oak, Chestnut, Deodar, and Maple trees.
● As you continue walking, witness the outstanding Dronagiri peak, a sight that will leave your heart numb.

Arrival at Gulling Top:
● Finally, reach the Gulling top, your home for the day.
● Set up camp here in tents and retire for the night under the star-lit sky.
● May this day be filled with breathtaking views and the serenity of nature! 🏔🌲🌟

DAY 3

Guling to Lower Khullara Camp/Taali:

Start Point: Guling
Distance: Approximately 6 kilometers
Duration: 6 to 7 hours

Description:
● Begin your day by trekking uphill from Guling.
● As you ascend, you will traverse pastures adorned in various shades of green, where shepherds tend to their sheep.
● The trail is steep, leading you through enchanting forests of Oak, Rhododendron (mostly pink and white), and Walnut trees.
● Amidst this natural beauty, feast your eyes on stunning views of the surrounding mountain ranges.
● Finally, arrive at the base camp—the Lower Khullara Camp/Taali Campsite.
● Here, you will settle down in your cozy tents for a well-deserved night rest.
● Remember, this is just one part of the incredible Pangarchulla Trek, which takes you through diverse landscapes, from lush greenery to snowy wonderlands. Enjoy the journey! 🏔🌿🏕

For more details about the entire trek, including other days and highlights, feel free to explore further! 🚶‍♂🌄

DAY 4

Itinerary for the Kuari Pass Trek.

Khulara to Kuari Pass and Back

Altitude: From Khulara (11,075 feet) to Kuari Pass (12,763 feet) and return.
Trekking Distance: Approximately 10 kilometers (7-8 hours of trekking).

Description:
● Early Morning Start: Begin your day with an early morning start from the Khulara base camp.
● Steep Ascent: Ascend through a steep trail, which may sometimes be covered with snow, adding an adventurous touch to your experience.
● Panoramic Views: As you climb, feast your eyes on panoramic views of the surrounding peaks, including Nanda Devi, Dronagiri, Kamet, and Trishul.
● Snow-Capped Landscapes: The trail takes you through enchanting rhododendron and oak forests, where you will witness various flora and fauna.
● Wildflower Canvas: Vibrant wildflowers, such as primroses and blue poppies, create a picturesque canvas against the backdrop of snow-capped peaks.
● Photographer Paradise: The serene meadows of Gorson Bugyal and Tali Top offer breathtaking views of the Himalayas, making it a photographer paradise.
● Return to Khulara: After reaching the Kuari Pass, retrace your steps back to the Khulara base camp.
● Meals: Enjoy your meals—breakfast, lunch, and dinner—while camping at Khulara.
● Remember, this day is the highlight of your trek, so embrace the adventure, capture the beauty, and savor the moments in the lap of the mighty Himalayas! 🏔❄

DAY 5

 Itinerary for the Pangarchulla Trek, which involves reaching the Pangarchulla Summit and returning to the Khullara Camps/Taali Camps:

Khullara Camps/Taali Camps to Pangarchulla Summit and Back
Altitude: From Khullara Camps/Taali Camps (approximately 11,100 feet) to the Pangarchulla Summit (15,096 feet) and return.
Trekking Distance: Approximately 14 kilometers (10-11 hours of trekking).

Description:
● Early Start: Begin your day with an early morning breakfast around 01:30 am.
● Summit Ascent: Embark on a challenging journey to the Pangarchulla Summit. The trail will test your endurance and determination.
● Stunning Views: As you climb higher, soak in the breathtaking views of the surrounding peaks, including Nanda Devi, Kamet, and Dronagiri.
● Achieve the Summit: Challenge yourself and reach the summit at an impressive altitude of 15,080 feet.
● 360-Degree Panorama: From the summit, enjoy a 360-degree panorama of the majestic Himalayan ranges and the valleys below.
● Return to Khullara: After savoring the accomplishment, retrace your steps back to the Khullara Camps/Taali Camps.
● Overnight Stay: Rest and rejuvenate in your tents, surrounded by the serene beauty of the mountains.
● Remember, this day is a culmination of your efforts, and standing atop the Pangarchulla Summit will reward you with unforgettable memories and awe-inspiring vistas! 🏔❄

DAY 6

Itinerary for the Pangarchulla Trek, as you journey from Taali Campsite/Khullara Camps to Lower Auli/Joshimath via the picturesque Gorson Bugyal:

Taali Campsite/Khullara Camps to Lower Auli/Joshimath via Gorson Bugyal
Distance: Approximately 13 kilometers
Duration: 11-12 hours

Description:
● Post Breakfast: Begin your day with a hearty breakfast at the campsite.
● Scenic Trek: Embark on a scenic trek, leaving behind the serene beauty of Taali Campsite/Khullara Camps.
● Gorson Bugyal: As you trek, immerse yourself in the stunning views of the lush meadows of Gorson Bugyal. These expansive grasslands are a treat for the eyes, surrounded by snow-capped peaks.
● Lower Auli/Joshimath: Continue your descent, and eventually, you will arrive at Lower Auli/Joshimath. The quaint town welcomes you with its charm and tranquility.
● Evening Leisure: The rest of the evening is yours to unwind, relax, and soak in the mountain vibes.
● Overnight Stay: Settle into your accommodation in Joshimath or Auli, where the crisp mountain air will lull you to sleep.
● Remember, every step of this trek is an adventure, and Lower Auli/Joshimath awaits with its own magic! 🏔🌿

DAY 7

Itinerary for your return journey from Joshimath/Lower Auli to Rishikesh:

Joshimath/Lower Auli to Rishikesh
Distance: Approximately 132 kilometers (82.4 miles)
Duration: Approximately 2 hours and 12 minutes12.

Pickup from Rishikesh:
● Early Morning Start: Begin your day early, soaking in the fresh mountain air.
● Scenic Drive: Embark on a scenic drive from Joshimath or Lower Auli back to Rishikesh.
● Mountain Vistas: As you wind through the Himalayan roads, enjoy the breathtaking vistas of valleys, rivers, and snow-capped peaks.
● En Route: Pass through towns like Devprayag, Srinagar, Rudraprayag, Karnaprayag, and Chamoli, each with its unique charm and significance.
● Spiritual Exploration: Along the way, take moments to connect with the spiritual aura of the region.
● Arrival in Rishikesh: By evening, you will arrive in Rishikesh, a hub of spirituality and adventure.
● Memories: Reflect on the memories of your trek, the majestic Himalayas, and the camaraderie of fellow trekkers.
● Remember, this journey is not just about distance; it is about the soulful connection you have forged with the mountains. Safe travels! 🏔🚗
