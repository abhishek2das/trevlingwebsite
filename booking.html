Get Some Important Answers

What do I need to carry?
Pack appropriate clothing based on the destination's climate and activities. Include comfortable walking shoes, chargers, adapters, power banks and any specialized attire needed for excursions such as hiking or safaris.
Do you provide local guides during the tours?
Yes, all our tours are accompanied by experienced local guides who are knowledgeable about the destinations, history, culture, and wildlife. They will enhance your travel experience with insightful commentary and personalized attention.
How concerned should I be about the altitude?
Altitude sickness can manifest as headaches, nausea, dizziness, fatigue, and difficulty breathing. If you experience any of these symptoms, talk to our crew present there immediately.
Are your tours suitable for families with children?
Many of our tours are family-friendly and suitable for travelers of all ages. We offer special activities and accommodations for families to ensure an enjoyable and memorable experience for everyone.
Can I customize my tour itinerary?
Absolutely! At Banjaarey Travels, we understand that every traveler is unique, with different interests, preferences, and schedules. We offer customizable tour options to suit your preferences, interests, and budget. Contact us to discuss your requirements, and we'll tailor a unique itinerary just for you.
If there is bad weather during the tour?
In case of inclement weather or unforeseen circumstances, we may need to modify the tour itinerary or reschedule certain activities for your safety and enjoyment. Our team will keep you informed and make alternative arrangements as needed.
Are your tours suitable for solo travelers?
Yes, many of our tours are designed to cater to solo travelers. We offer group tours where you can join other like-minded travelers, as well as private tours for a more personalized experience.
Are there any hidden fees or extra charges I should be aware of?
We strive to be transparent with our pricing, and there are no hidden fees or surprise charges. Any optional activities or additional services not included in the tour package will be clearly communicated to you beforehand.
