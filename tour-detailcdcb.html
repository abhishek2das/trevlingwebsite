Rann of Kutch

₹8,999/Per Person
Duration 4 Days / 3 Nights
Difficulty Easy-Moderate
Group Size Any
Weather Cool
Itinerary

DAY 1

Day 1 of your Kutch trip:

● Kutch Trip Begins from Mumbai / Surat / Vadodra / Ahmedabad:
● 3:15 PM: Participants from Dadar, Mumbai, catch train number 20907 Sayaji Express to Bhuj.
● Next Day (Day 2) by 6:30 AM: Arrive at Bhuj Railway station.
● Get ready for an exciting adventure in the mesmerizing Rann of Kutch! 🏜

DAY 2

Day 2 of your Kutch trip:

Cultural Morning:
● 06:30 AM: Begin your day with excitement! Our team will pick you up from Bhuj Railway station, airport, or bus station.
● 07:30 AM: Enjoy a wholesome breakfast to fuel your adventure.

Kala Dungar (Black Hill):
● 10:00 AM: Embark on a scenic drive to Kala Dungar. As you ascend, the landscape unfolds before you, revealing panoramic views of the Rann of Kutch.
● India-Pakistan Border View: At Kala Dungar, you will get a glimpse of the India-Pakistan border. Stand at this significant point and reflect on the shared history and culture.

Luxurious Stay and Local Cuisine:
● 01:00 PM: Check in to our luxurious accommodation. Take a moment to unwind and soak in the surroundings.
● Authentic Local Food: Lunchtime! Relish an authentic local meal, savoring the flavors of Kutch.

Sunset Spectacle:
● 04:00 PM: Venture out to witness a majestic sunset. The vast expanse of the Rann bathed in warm hues is a sight to behold.

Rann Cultural Program and Photography:
● 07:00 PM: Return to your base and immerse yourself in the Rann cultural program/festival. Experience the vibrant music, dance, and traditions of this unique region. Capture memories through your lens.
● Traditional Tents: As night falls, retire to your cozy traditional tents. The desert silence and star-studded sky await you.

Moonlit Desert Exploration:
● 10:30 PM: For the night owls, step out after dinner. The moonlight transforms the white desert into a magical wonderland. Feel free to explore or simply rest under the celestial canopy.
● May this day be filled with cultural immersion, natural beauty, and unforgettable moments! 🏜🌅🌕

DAY 3

Day 3 of your Kutch trip:

Sunrise at Rann of Kutch:
● 06:00 AM – 08:00 AM: Rise early to witness the first rays of the sunrise over the vast Rann of Kutch. Let the golden light paint the salt desert in hues of orange and pink.

Breakfast and Check-Out:
● 08:00 AM – 09:30 AM: Enjoy a leisurely breakfast and check out from your accommodation.

Drive Back to Bhuj:
● 09:30 AM – 1:00 PM: Begin your drive back to Bhuj. Along the way, savor lunch en route and take in the scenic views.

Local Sightseeing in Bhuj:
● 01:00 PM – 7:30 PM: Explore the local attractions in Bhuj:
● Aadhiyogi Statue: Marvel at the impressive Aadhiyogi Statue, a symbol of spirituality and strength.
● Khari Nadi: Visit the serene Khari Nadi, where the river flows gently through the landscape.
● Kutch Museum: Immerse yourself in the rich history and culture of Kutch at the Kutch Museum.
● Aaina Mahal: Step into the past at the Aaina Mahal, a palace adorned with intricate mirrors and exquisite architecture.
● Chhatedi: Pay your respects at the ancient Chhatedi, a cenotaph complex.
● Local Shopping: Explore the bustling markets of Bhuj and shop for handicrafts, textiles, and souvenirs.
● Swaminarayan Temple: Visit the serene Swaminarayan Temple and soak in its spiritual ambiance.

Drop-off at Bhuj:
● 9:30 PM: Conclude your day with a drop-off at Bhuj Railway station, airport, or bus station.
● May your exploration continue to be filled with wonder and discovery! 🏜🌅🕌

DAY 4

Day 4 of your Kutch trip:

Bhuj to Ahmedabad:
● Morning Departure: After a delightful stay in Bhuj, bid farewell to this enchanting land.
● Drive to Ahmedabad: Embark on your journey from Bhuj to Ahmedabad, the vibrant city known for its rich history, culture, and architecture.
● Scenic Drive: Enjoy the changing landscapes as you traverse the roads connecting these two cities.

Mumbai Participants Return:
● Borivali Arrival (1:10 PM): Participants from Mumbai will reach Borivali by 1:10 PM. Memories of the Rann of Kutch will linger in their hearts.
● Dadar Arrival (1:50 PM): For those heading to Dadar, the train will bring them back by 1:50 PM.
● May this journey leave you with cherished memories and a longing to return to the magical Kutch! 🏜🚂❤
